Measurement families follow the information block definition used in the analysis and the released source documentation. x1 and x7 are the two pre-test date fields retained for age derivation, and x7 precedes x8 for all 100 characterized cells. Variables used in the deployable blocks are x2 to x6 and x9 to x11. The pack yield summary sb is excluded from all models because it reproduces the post-characterization fraction of cells at or above 70 percent capacity.
